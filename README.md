Heritage Brand Evolution

Brand: Raw Mango
Project Type: Strategic Brand Evolution
Role: Creative & Digital Brand Strategist

This project explores how Raw Mango could evolve its digital presence while preserving its cultural depth and quiet luxury positioning.

Raw Mango is deeply rooted in Indian textile heritage and artisanal craftsmanship. Rather than proposing louder marketing or trend-led growth, I focused on structured storytelling as a strategic lever for digital visibility.

The central strategic question guiding this project was:

How can Raw Mango increase digital discoverability without compromising its restraint, cultural authority, and timeless positioning?

The proposed solution introduces brand-led narrative frameworks, seasonless storytelling pillars, and curated digital platforms (including Pinterest) to expand reach while maintaining exclusivity.
